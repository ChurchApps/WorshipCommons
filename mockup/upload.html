Share a song

Give the church something to sing

Four quick steps: the song, its files, what you're giving, and your word that it's yours to give. A human reviews every song before it goes live — usually within a few days. Prefer to release a song without uploading it? Copy the release notice.

1The song

What a worship leader needs to find it and decide if it fits Sunday.

Paste plain lyrics or ChordPro. We generate the chord chart, lead sheet, and slides from this.

2Files

Optional — but a demo recording is the single best thing you can give a worship leader deciding at 10pm on a Thursday.

Demo recording Drop an MP3 or WAV, or click to choose · a phone recording is fine
Sheet music Lead sheet or vocal score · PDF or MusicXML
Multitracks ZIP of stems — one WAV or MP3 per part, every file starting at bar 1 · include click & guide if you have them

Upload stems once, in the recorded key — we render other keys for churches automatically.

3What you're giving

Both options make the song free for worship forever. They differ in what you keep.

4Your word

U.S.-style societies leave you free to give your own song away. Many societies elsewhere (GEMA and others) take over your performance rights when you join — if that's you, check your membership terms before sharing, or this grant may not be yours to make.

Reviewed by a human before it appears — usually within a few days.
