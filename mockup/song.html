Every Valley

Words and music by Miriam Okafor · 2023

"Every valley shall be lifted up, and every mountain and hill be made low." — Isaiah 40:4

Key D 72 BPM 4/4 Advent Comfort Hope
Any key, no permission needed

Verse 1

D G D

Every valley shall be lifted,

Bm A

every mountain be made low;

D G D

every crooked road made straight now —

Em A D

where the Lord will surely go.

Chorus

G A D

Prepare the way of the Lord,

G A Bm

prepare the way of the Lord —

G Em

all flesh shall see it together:

G A D

prepare the way of the Lord.

Verse 2

D G D

Grass will wither, flowers falter,

Bm A

all our glory fades like breath;

D G D

but the word of God unchanging

Em A D

stands when nothing else is left.

Bridge

Bm G

He will feed his flock like a shepherd,

D A

he will gather the lambs in his arms.
